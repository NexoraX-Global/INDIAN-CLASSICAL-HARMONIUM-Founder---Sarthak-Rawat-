HARMONIUM LEARNING ACADEMY & INTERACTIVE RAGA TUTOR

1. DHAMAN (BELLOWS & AIR RESERVOIR)

Why No Sound Plays Without Pumping

An authentic Indian harmonium is a free-reed aerophone. Holding keys opens air valves underneath the keybed, but no sound is produced unless the internal reservoir is pressurized.

Use your left thumb to drag/pump the wooden paddle on the left, enable Mic Blow to breathe into your phone, use Tilt Rock, or toggle Auto-Pump when practicing two-handed exercises.

2. TRIPLE BRASS REED STOPS (KALI / SAFED)

Bass (Kharaj), Male (Nar) & Female (Mada)

Pulling out the large brass knobs opens air channels into three distinct banks of brass reeds inside the Burmese teak soundboard:

  • BASS (16ft): Sub-octave reed giving deep Palitana cathedral resonance.
  • MALE (8ft): Primary fundamental concert pitch with rich nasal warmth.
  • FEMALE (8ft +3.5c): Slightly detuned unison reed creating the signature shimmering Kolkata acoustic chorus.
  • TREMOLO: Engages a spinning air baffle for expressive vibrato.
3. JOD (MECHANICAL OCTAVE COUPLER)

Automatic Upper-Octave Doubling

When the COUPLER (JOD) switch is active, pressing any key physically pulls down the key exactly 12 semitones (one octave) higher, doubling the richness of every note.

4. SCALE CHANGER & SURPETI DRONES

Instant Vocalist Pitch Matching

Slide the Scale Changer (-4 to +4 semitones) so you can always play using familiar Safed 1 (C) fingerings while shifting the acoustic pitch to Kali 1 (C#), Kali 2 (D#), or any vocalist's tonic. Pull the SA, PA, or MA small knobs for continuous background drone reeds.

BEGINNER ALANKAR EXERCISE

Alankar 1: Shuddha Swara Ascent (Bilawal Scale)

Foundation Finger Independence

The foundational seven-note scale (Saptak) using all natural (Shuddha) white keys from Madhya Sa (C4) to Taar Sa (C5). Practice with smooth bellows pressure so every swara sings at equal volume.

AROHA (Ascending): सा रे ग म प ध नि सां (C4 D4 E4 F4 G4 A4 B4 C5)
AVAROHA (Descending): सां नि ध प म ग रे सा (C5 B4 A4 G4 F4 E4 D4 C4)
PAKAD (Signature Phrase): सा रे ग म प, म ग रे सा
VADI / SAMVADI: Vadi: Dha (A4) • Samvadi: Ga (E4)
TECHNIQUE 1: BELLOWS BREATHING (SWAS-PRASWAS)

Expressive Dynamics & Avoiding Pitch Sag

Watch the PSI Air Gauge in the top HUD. Pumping harder swells volume and opens the acoustic low-pass filter from 400Hz up to 4500Hz, making the brass reeds shimmer. Letting pressure fall near 0% causes notes to organically starve and sag in pitch—just like a real acoustic bellows running out of air.

TECHNIQUE 2: MEEND, GAMAK & KAN-SWAR

Glissando & Microtonal Pitch Bending

Keep GLISSANDO LOCKED enabled above the keys and slide your right-hand fingers smoothly across adjacent keys for rapid classical Taans. To bend a note microtonally (±100 cents) for Meend, slide your finger vertically up/down on any held key or drag across the Kan-Swar Ribbon.

TECHNIQUE 3: DESKTOP KEYBOARD SHORTCUTS

Play on Laptop or External Keyboard

If testing on a computer: press Spacebar repeatedly to pump the bellows, use Z–M for Mandra Saptak, A–L / Q–P for Madhya & Taar Saptak, and Shift to toggle Auto-Pump.

TROUBLESHOOTING & MOBILE AUDIO TIPS

Silent Notes or Sensor Permissions

  • No sound when pressing keys? Check the Air Reservoir gauge! Either pump the left bellows paddle or click AUTO-PUMP.
  • Mic Blow not responding? Ensure you run via HTTPS or localhost and grant microphone permission when prompted.
  • Audio crackle on older phones? Open Settings (&gear;) and switch Rendering Mode to Battery Saver.
